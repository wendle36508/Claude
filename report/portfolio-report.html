How this portfolio gets built

Every position starts as a researched thesis, not a screener hit. The same loop that sizes today's positions is meant to get smarter about which signals to trust as real performance accrues.

01

Research signal

Live search across filings, earnings, insider activity, and sector/macro news — not just a 10-K skim.

02

Thesis

Each name gets an explicit bull case and bear case, dated and sourced, logged before capital is committed.

03

Position sizing

Weights are signal-informed within a risk-managed sleeve — a name with a flagged risk signal gets sized down, not skipped.

04

Calibration

Realized returns get checked back against conviction and individual signals, so the process prunes what didn't actually predict anything.

Why core-satellite

A concentrated basket of AI-infrastructure bets is a research thesis, not a portfolio — it wins big if the theme plays out and gets hit hard if sentiment turns, with no ballast either way. Wealth management is the opposite instinct: client capital is sized so no single call, however good the research, can do outsized damage. The 50% core sleeve (broad equity, bonds, cash) is that ballast, funded first and rebalanced independently of how any satellite thesis is performing.

Sizing within the satellite sleeve is also signal-informed rather than equal-weight: SNDK carries the smallest satellite position specifically because its own logged signal flags a blow-off-top risk profile (up roughly 50x in a year), while CRWV and NBIS — with stronger backlog and growth signals — carry the largest.

Herfindahl-Hirschman Index 0.181

Sum of squared position weights — lower means more diversified. 0.181 is roughly equivalent to holding 5.5 equal-weight positions.

Caveat: HHI treats each position as independent. The four satellite names are all exposed to the same AI-infrastructure theme and move together more than the number alone suggests — real diversification here comes from the core/satellite split, not the position count.

What happens next

Daily research pass — a scheduled check re-searches every name for material news and logs only genuinely new, sourced signals.
Calibration reporting — once enough snapshots accrue, conviction levels and individual signal types get checked against realized returns — the honest version of "which of my ideas actually worked."
Benchmark tracking — portfolio return gets tracked against SPY, not just in isolation.
