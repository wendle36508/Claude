Portfolio

Built with a Python/SQLite research tracker (wealth_lab). Every stock's 0-100 score comes from sourced, dated signals (each bullish or bearish, with a link) averaged and decayed by age, then shrunk toward 50 by how much evidence backs it - a thin, one-signal read can't score as extreme as a well-supported one. Expected range and risk metrics (volatility/Sharpe/drawdown/beta) are computed separately, not folded into the score. This page connects live to the deployed API when it's reachable (see the line under the title) and falls back to a static snapshot otherwise - see the Score Detail and Recent News sections on any ticker for exactly how current its evidence is.
